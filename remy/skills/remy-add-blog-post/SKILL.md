Add a blog post: a new item in the Blog collection, listed on /blog with its own page from the /blog/:slug page.

## Working from outside Framer

- Read remy-base first: it has the Framer MCP setup and the rules for every Remy task. If you can't reach the project, ask the owner to connect Framer and share the project link.
- Names in this skill are exact names in the project. Find each by name; if one is missing, ask, and never build a replacement.
- Framer MCP edits the owner's main version directly unless you work on a branch; if you use one, give the owner its name and link. Never publish, and merge only when the owner asks.
- Images come from the owner, as public links or dropped into their fields in Framer. Never generate or download images unless asked.
- If your tools can't do a step, give the owner the exact steps and list them in your report.
- Check your work in the project's preview at 1200, 810, about 700 and 390 px wide and in dark mode. If you can't see it, say so in your report.

## 1. Ask the owner

Use anything they've already shared, then ask for the rest in one numbered list, with an example for each:

1. Title (Write it down before you draw it)
2. Summary: one sentence on what the post is about, shown on the card, under the title and in search results (Why every project starts with a page of plain sentences.)
3. Date and topic: Process, Systems or Craft (March 3, 2026; Process)
4. The post itself, or notes to shape into one
5. A cover image, with a few words on what it shows. A 1200 × 630 social image is optional.

Wait for answers. With no cover image, ask once more.

## 2. Build the item

- **Title:** sets the Slug, so keep it unique.
- **Date and Topic:** the date they give, or today's. A topic outside the three is a new option: ask first, and give it a Filter Tab on /blog, set up like the others.
- **Summary:** one sentence, under about 120 characters; it's also the search description.
- **Cover image:** 4:3, at least 2400 px wide, with one plain sentence of alt text on what it shows.
- **Body:** the owner's text in their words and order, fixing only typos and slips; from notes, finished paragraphs that add nothing they didn't give you. Paragraphs and Heading 2 subheads only; the Body isn't styled for lists, quotes or images, so ask before adding one. No empty paragraphs between paragraphs; one empty paragraph before each Heading 2, as in the sample posts. The title is the page's only Heading 1.
- **Social image:** the owner's 1200 × 630 image, or else the Cover image.

If you can't add an image, fill in everything else and tell the owner which image goes in which field. Leave the item live, not a draft.

## Rules

- Use only the owner's words, facts and images. Never invent quotes, clients or results, and never generate images.
- Add a new item; never rewrite a sample post into the owner's. When you finish, say how many samples are left and offer to delete them once the owner has a few posts of their own (Sample content in remy-base).
- Write in the voice from section 07 of /design-system, then reread it as remy-remove-ai-writing describes.

## Done when

- The post shows on /blog under All and under its topic tab, newest first by Date.
- Its page reads well at every size, and More notes at the bottom shows other posts.
- You've listed anything missing, such as a cover image, alt text or a date.
