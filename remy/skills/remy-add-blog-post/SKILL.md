Add a blog post: a new item in the Blog collection, listed on /blog with its own page from the /blog/:slug page.

## Working from outside Framer

- Read remy-base first: it has the Framer MCP setup and the rules for every Remy task. If you can't reach the project, ask the owner to connect Framer and share the project link.
- Names in this skill are exact names in the project. Find each by name; if one is missing, ask, and never build a replacement.
- Framer MCP edits the owner's main version directly unless you work on a branch (if the owner's plan has them); if you use one, give the owner its name and link. Never publish, and merge only when the owner asks.
- Images come from the owner, as public links or dropped into their fields in Framer. Never generate or download images unless asked.
- If your tools can't do a step, give the owner the exact steps and list them in your report.
- Check your work at the widths and modes remy-base lists, and follow its Before you report steps. If you can't see something, say so in your report.

## 1. Ask the owner

Use anything they've already shared, then ask for the rest in one numbered list, with an example for each. Where their notes allow, put your best guess in the list ("4. Topic: Process?") so the owner can just say yes.

1. Title (Write it down before you draw it)
2. Summary: one sentence on what the post is about, shown on the card, under the title and in search results (Why every project starts with a page of plain sentences.)
3. Date, written out even for "today" so they can catch a wrong day (March 3, 2026?). Count today in the Nav clock's time zone, not your own or Framer's.
4. Topic: Process, Systems or Craft
5. The post itself, or notes to shape into one
6. A cover image, with a few words on what it shows. A 1200 × 630 social image is optional.

If the notes are thin (the sample posts run several hundred words), add two or three questions drawn from them that would give the post more to say, such as what taught them this or what they do differently now. A short post is fine if they'd rather not add more; padding isn't.

Send the list and wait for the reply before you change anything in Framer, even if your tools' guidance says to ask only when something is unclear: a wrong date, topic or cover is the first thing visitors see on /blog. Go straight to building only when the owner has already given everything, a written-out date included. With no cover image, ask once more. If they give no post or notes, ask once for a few points, and don't write it for them: anything you make up would read as their own experience. If they still say go ahead, add the item as a draft with placeholders in square brackets ([YOUR POST]).

## 2. Build the item

- **Title:** sets the Slug, so keep it unique.
- **Date and Topic:** as confirmed. A topic outside the three is a new option: ask first, give it a Filter Tab on /blog set up like the others, and offer to update the /blog intro if it names the topics.
- **Summary:** one sentence, under about 120 characters; it's also the search description.
- **Cover image:** 4:3, at least 2400 px wide, with one plain sentence of alt text on what it shows.
- **Body:** as below. The title is the page's only Heading 1.
- **Social image:** the owner's 1200 × 630 image, or else the Cover image.

If you can't add an image (a link that doesn't open, or isn't an image), fill in everything else and tell the owner which image goes in which field. Without a cover, the post page and its card show a stand-in picture that visitors would take for the post's own, so leave the item as a draft until it has one, and tell the owner how to add it and switch the post live. Otherwise leave it live.

### The body

- **The owner's own post:** keep their words, spelling and order, fixing only typos and slips, and suggest any other change instead of making it.
- **From notes:** write finished paragraphs, not the notes on separate lines. Lead with the note that carries the main point, then order the rest so each leads to the next. Join related points into paragraphs of two to four sentences, with words that say how they connect (so, then, which means); use "because" only where the owner gave the reason. Every sentence should trace back to a note, with no new facts, feelings or examples, and every note should be used unless it doesn't fit (say which in your report).
- **Subheads:** a longer post with distinct parts gets Heading 2 subheads, plain statements like the samples' (Sentences catch what sketches hide). A short post needs none.
- Paragraphs and Heading 2 subheads only; the Body isn't styled for lists, quotes or images, so ask before adding one.

### Spacing

Each empty paragraph in the Body shows as a gap on the page, so follow the sample posts' pattern:

- Start and end with a paragraph of text, never an empty one.
- No empty paragraphs between paragraphs; exactly one before each Heading 2.
- Stray line breaks in the text you send can become empty paragraphs. After saving, read the Body back block by block and fix anything that breaks the pattern.

## Rules

- Use only the owner's words, facts and images. Never invent quotes, clients or results, and never generate images.
- Add a new item; never rewrite a sample post into the owner's. When you finish, say how many samples are left and offer to delete them once the owner has a few posts of their own (Sample content in remy-base).
- Write in the voice from section 07 of /design-system, then reread it as remy-remove-ai-writing describes.

## Done when

- The post shows on /blog under All and under its topic tab, newest first by Date, unless it's a draft.
- Its page reads well at every size, its Body follows the spacing pattern, and More notes at the bottom shows other posts.
- Your report gives the date you used and lists anything missing (cover image, alt text, a 1200 × 630 social image), any guesses the owner hasn't confirmed, and whether the Written by byline on the post page still shows the sample designer.
